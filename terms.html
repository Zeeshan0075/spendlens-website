9. Automatic capture — bank messages, notifications, screenshots and Apple Pay

The App can capture transactions from your bank's SMS alerts, from notifications posted by a bank or card app you choose, from screenshots of bank notifications you share to it, and from in-store Apple Pay payments. If you use any of these, you understand and agree that:

  • Every route is opt-in and off until you set it up. Each relies on an iOS Shortcuts automation that you configure manually — a message automation for SMS, a Notification automation for a bank app's alerts (iOS 27 and later), or a Wallet automation for Apple Pay.
  • When Smart Parsing is enabled, the captured message or notification text is sent over an encrypted connection to a cloud processing service — routed through Cloudflare and processed by Google's Gemini model via Vertex AI — to extract the transaction. When Smart Parsing is disabled, no message or notification text is transmitted.
  • For screenshots, text is extracted from the image on your device using Apple's Vision framework, and only that text is transmitted. The image itself is not transmitted and is deleted after the text is read.
  • Apple Pay capture is processed entirely on your device. The merchant, amount and card name that iOS hands to the App are not transmitted to the Developer or to anyone else, and this route does not use Smart Parsing. Apple's Wallet provides this for in-store taps only, not for online or in-app Apple Pay.
  • Capture is not guaranteed to be accurate. By default, every captured transaction waits for you to confirm it. If you switch on automatic approval of high-confidence captures, transactions are added to your expenses without review, and you are responsible for checking what was added.
  • Where the same purchase is reported by more than one route — an Apple Pay tap, a bank notification and a bank SMS, for instance — the App combines them into a single expense. This matching can be wrong in either direction: where it has combined two genuinely separate purchases you can split them apart again in the App, and where it is unsure it asks you first.
  • You are responsible for ensuring the iOS Shortcuts automations work correctly on your device.
  • The App cannot read SMS or notifications directly under any circumstances. Only the messages and alerts your automations forward, or screenshots you share, are ever processed.
